Infrastructure

A drainage network to end decades of flooding

After a needs assessment showed drainage, not resurfacing, was the constituency's most critical gap, a planned ten kilometre inner-roads contract was converted into a major inner-community drainage project covering Mamobi, Kawukudi, Accra New Town, the Kwatso electoral area and surrounding neighbourhoods. A local contractor was deliberately chosen so the community stays engaged in the works.

Youth & Sports

135 youth groups kitted out

In November 2024 the MP's office distributed sets of jerseys and footballs to 135 community youth groups across Ayawaso North, a constituency that has produced professionals including Majeed Ashimeru and Abdul Manaf Nurudeen. It followed an earlier round in May 2024 that equipped nine football clubs and added a cash prize.

November 2024 · A proper training pitch is the next commitment

Digital Skills

AI training for the youth, through to graduation

His Youth in AI and Digital Skills Training Workshop packed a hall in the constituency with young people at laptops for fourteen days, learning practical, future facing tools: AI, Canva, Wix and CapCut. The training was free, open to the youth of Ayawaso North, and carried through to a graduation for its first cohort of AI trainees.

Parliament

Finance, roads and transport

An accountant and energy economist by training, he brings technical weight to the Finance Committee and the Roads and Transport Committee, the two committees where a constituency like Ayawaso North either gets budgeted for, or gets forgotten. He is also active on energy, defence and interior matters.

7th, 8th and 9th Parliaments of the Fourth Republic

National Service

Deputy Minister for Tourism, Culture and Creative Arts

Appointed to the Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Creative Arts, he carries a brief with real economic upside for young Ghanaians: creative industries, cultural heritage and the tourism value chain, approached with a development finance lens.

Appointed 2025

Advocacy

Naming the gaps out loud

He has publicly pressed the case that Kwatso and Mamobi East still have no government school, and that the constituency lacks a fire station, an ambulance post and a community centre. Putting those absences on the record is the first step to getting them funded.

Ongoing

Mandate

A mandate that keeps growing

Elected in 2016 with 22,144 votes (59.97%), returned in 2020 with 28,207 votes (62.66%), and re-elected again in 2024 for a third term. Few things speak louder about a representative than a constituency that keeps sending him back.
